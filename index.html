Offline Music Player - Support

Welcome! This page provides help and support for Offline Music Player.

How to Use

  1. Connect your iPhone and computer to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open the app and go to Import via Wi-Fi.
  3. Enter the shown URL in your browser and upload your songs.
  4. Enjoy offline playback anytime, anywhere.

FAQ

Q: Do I need internet to play music?
A: No, once songs are imported, you can listen completely offline.

Q: What audio formats are supported?
A: MP3, AAC, WAV, and more.
